## ☕ The 4-Bag Contrasting Lineup

### 1. [The Classic](https://www.blackwhiteroasters.com/products/the-classic) — $21.00 (12oz)
- **Category:** Comforting Blend (Upper-Medium Exploration)
- **Origin & Components:** Colombia (*Las Nubes Washed*) & Ethiopia (*Aricha Washed*)
- **Process:** Washed
- **Roast Level:** Medium-Dark (Specialty Upper-Medium)
- **Tasting Notes:** Caramelized sugars, subtle stone fruits, black cherry, milk chocolate, balanced acidity.
- **Why It's Included:** Maintains the ~75/25 single-origin-to-blend balance while exploring the upper end of the medium roast spectrum without sacrificing specialty sweetness.
- **Recommended Brew Methods:**
  - **Espresso (Milk Drinks):** Ideal for cortados and lattes—rich chocolate and caramel notes cut cleanly through milk.
  - **Oxo Zouper (Batch Brew):** Great for a cozy, crowd-pleasing larger batch.

---

### 2. [Ushirika - Washed](https://www.blackwhiteroasters.com/products/r-ushirika-washed) — $26.00 (12oz)
- **Category:** Clean Washed "Daily Drinker"
- **Origin:** Nyeri, Kenya (*Othaya Farmers Cooperative*, 1600–1900 MASL)
- **Varieties:** SL28, SL34, Batian, Ruiru 11
- **Process:** Washed
- **Roast Level:** Light
- **Tasting Notes:** Date, milk chocolate, orange zest acidity, baked apples sprinkled with cinnamon.
- **Why It's Included:** First Kenyan coffee since October 2025. Unlike sharp or savory Kenyan profiles, this lot offers rounded citrus-zest acidity and warm, autumn-spiced fruit notes—providing a clean washed contrast to the previous Passenger Coffee Ethiopian and Colombian lots.
- **Recommended Brew Methods:**
  - **Pourover:** Highlights the delicate spiced baked-apple and date sweetness.
  - **Oxo Zouper (Batch Brew):** Clean, sweet daily filter coffee.

---

### 3. [Gamatui - Supernatural](https://www.blackwhiteroasters.com/products/r-gamatui-supernatural) — $28.00 (12oz)
- **Category:** New-Origin Natural Bridge ("Mild Funk")
- **Origin:** Kapchorwa, Mt. Elgon, Uganda (*Gamatui Washing Station / Sipi Falls*, 1600–1900 MASL)
- **Varieties:** SL Varieties
- **Process:** Natural & Anaerobic Natural
- **Roast Level:** Light
- **Tasting Notes:** Fresh concord grapes, praline pecan, milk chocolate bar sprinkled with orange zest.
- **Why It's Included:** Introduces Uganda as a brand-new origin in the purchase log. Sits right in the middle of Black & White's spectrum, bridging nutty milk-chocolate comfort with vibrant concord grape fruitiness.
- **Recommended Brew Methods:**
  - **Straight Espresso:** Pulls syrupy shots with punchy concord grape and chocolate notes.
  - **Pourover:** Showcases the layered grape and praline sweetness.

---

### 4. [Diego Horta - Anaerobic Natural](https://www.blackwhiteroasters.com/products/r-diego-horta-anaerobic-natural) — $26.00 (12oz)
- **Category:** The "Fun, Different Cup" (Experimental Fermentation)
- **Origin:** Santa Maria, Huila, Colombia (*Finca El Rincon*, processed by *La Palma y El Tucán*, 1700 MASL)
- **Varieties:** Caturra, Castillo
- **Process:** Anaerobic Natural
- **Roast Level:** Light
- **Tasting Notes:** Black cherry cordial, marzipan, spiced orange, craft chocolate finish.
- **Why It's Included:** Provides a direct processing contrast to the clean washed Colombian (*Divino Niño*) from the September Passenger order. Processed by La Palma y El Tucán—pioneers of anaerobic fermentation in Colombia—using sealed-tank fermentation with no added fruits or spices.
- **Recommended Brew Methods:**
  - **Pourover:** Best for experiencing how the cup shifts from boozy cherry cordial when hot to marzipan and craft chocolate as it cools.
  - **Straight Espresso:** For an intense, fruit-forward experimental shot.
